# 2026 Painting Competition

The 2026 Painting Competition took place from February 2nd to March 4th, 2026. 

> Good evening Drehmari!
> 
> Throughout the course of [[Drehmal v22|2.2]]'s development, we ran a few different build competitions to get some community creations into the map. For [[Drehmal v23|2.3]], we're doing a painting competition! We want to replace all of the vanilla Minecraft paintings with our own Drehmal-themed ones, which is a big endeavor - one that you can help us with!
> This is a chance to have your artwork featured in Drehmal, and earn a place in our credits alongside previous community contributors.

![[2026_Painting_Wall.png]]

More than 100 community paintings were submitted, and of those, there were 14 finalist artists with a total of 38 paintings selected for inclusion in 2.3, several of which are visible above.

The list of winners is: Arkwhisper, Av'Parhaln, foodcubed, Kyte, the_gamer (maelhrizz), PocketApple8104, Prox, Bird / Pundus, Scorp, Shotnoms, No, 1.22474487139..., and IsoΦ 

A gallery of all 38 winning pieces is available on the website at <https://www.drehmal.net/paintingcomp2026>.
