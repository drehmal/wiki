# Letters to the Sky

!!! warning "Side Content"
    This page concerns a piece of side content from the official Drehmal development team which is not a part of any mainline Drehmal map release. It does, however, come from the Drehmal dev team, and can likely be considered fully canon.

--8<-- "am2_spoiler.md"

This book is a lore book only present in the [[Archived_Memory_2|Archived Memory 2]] teaser map, located on Zedari Island just off the coast of [[Sahd]]. It can be found in a chest inside the temple of the Skyborn monks. The book is actually a collection of four letters addressed to whom the monks venerate: Zedoh, Sky Supernal.

Furthermore, this book is signed by "Skyborn Raema" and the item itself comes with the following lore-text: "A collection of four handwritten letters, left in a loose bundle. The ink on the last entry has only just dried."

## Transcription
> Day 1
> ---
> 
> -------------------
>
> First comes the sky.
>
> You, the starlight at time's beginning.
>
> You, the fire that burned the world before.
>
> You, a sign I have awaited my entire life.
>
> And here you are.
>
> I have always adored you, Zedoh. As a child, before I knew that you were you, I would spend clear nights stargazing in the eastern highlands. Carving sticks through the mud, recreating the patterns formed by the brightest stars, and committing my favorites to memory. When that lost its novelty, I imagined that each star had an internal pattern that I could not view through their light. They were stunning, they had to be—but they were not for my eyes. With time, the thought became agonizing. The intricaies of the patterns of patterns I would never know.
>
> I wondered if anyone had ever grown tall enough, flown high enough, to see the greater truth. To know how the stars feel.
>
> My life changed the day I became Skyborn, when the brothers of the monastery revealed to me who you really were.
>
> "We turn our eyes to the stars, and they watch back, for they are The First made tangible." Elder Yun recited this wisdom with such... formality. As if the beauty of that statement had drifted to the back of his mind and faded over so many decades lived. I spent more time worrying the same would happen to me than I did thinking about the rest of my initiation. There wasn't a cloud in the sky all day.
>
> Two hours after dusk, as my new brothers rested their heads, I took my chance. I remember noting the softness of the ground. The coolness of the island air. The stars' intensity. The after three, or twenty, or a thousand moments beneath the constellations I knew so well... it clicked. The night sky is a miracle. Ten thousand dazzling eyes in their places all across the firmament. All come from the same point above, and then converge again upon the world. On me. The stars were—had always been—the points where you looked back at me.
>
> I was seen. Known. Loved.
>
> Not a waking hour of my new life has passed without a moment taken to peer upward. The watch is my duty, yes, but I perform it with a certain... selfishness. To ensure that you are still there. Still as fantastic as you were on that magical night four decades ago, the first time I felt the stars. Despite everything, you always are.
>
> But tonight the air felt different. The darkness was weaker. And as I turned my eye to the Golden Moon, I understood why. A new light.
>
> Never in my life have I seen you as gorgeous as you are tonight.
>
>
> Day 2 
> ---
>
> -------------------
> 
> It's been over a decade since your great southwestern eye closed, but I remember it as freshly as a would suffered yesterday.
>
> We tried our best to rationalize it. Did you have other matters to attend to, beyond our island? Beyond this realm? Perhaps you were hurt, or... trying to warn us of something. Questions turned to rituals, as we scrambled to regain your trust, to heal you, to grant you our own strength. I lit you a candle every night that year. I couldn't bear the thought of one less light in the world.
>
> And then it happened again. And again.
>
> When no answers came, concern morphed into frustration. I will admit—for a time, I was angry. That bitter flavor of anger which arises from fear, the mortifying notion that you would no longer be with us. That I would have to watch your lights fade out, once by one, left to wallow below an eternal void.
>
> But I understand now.
>
> Cyclicality does not apply exclusively to the birth and deaath of worlds. The Great Cycle encompasses all things, of course, but all things make up many cycles of their own.
>
> The patterns within the patterns.
>
> The inside of the star.
>
> The lights we have lost will never leave my heart, but it is time to move on. In this new form, as you have ordained, they will live on. I will burn no candles tonight.
>
> When your new creation is ready to share with the world, trust that I will be the first to lay eyes on it.
>
>
> Day 3
> ---
> 
> -------------------
> 
> It is getting rather late. I have done very little today but watch your admirable progress.
>
> Doubts have arisen within our ranks. Young Brother Aren pulled me aside this morning to express his.
>
> "Elder Raema", he began, attempting to project as much conviction as his frail chest could muster. "This... thing is like nothing we have ever seen, and Zedoh is the pinnacle of consistency! What if it is not a holy creation, but another sickness? An instrument of the Great Destroyer? Are we not due to fall in the Cycle of War?
>
> I took a deep breath so as not to smack the boy upside his head, then spoke. "An alteration in Zedoh's form has only ever signified change of the highest order." He appeared unconvinced.
>
> "You", I continued, "have spent too much time reading, and not enough watching. The Earth-Shaker is not coming for us. Be grateful that you may see the Great Cycle rise with your own eyes."
>
> Three separate times did I watch Aren form a rebuttal, only to bite his tongue. Relenting, he offered a half-hearted apology and suffled away. Nobody has spoken a word to me since.
>
> It has always been a struggle, inspiring true devotion in my brothers. I cannot make them feel the faith as I do.
>
> How could I? A feeling can only be learned from experience.
>
> I need you to show them.
>
> Please.
>
> 
> Day 4
> ---
> 
> -------------------
>
> I thought this morning was your answer. Impossible might and grace, dancing from the world's apex down to the horizon. A most miraculous birth to usher in the coming rise.
>
> I... I do not know what to think now.
>
> I hope that you are not upset with me. I am sorry for misjudging. I trust that you will save us once again.
>
> But I cannot ignore this progression. The land has made its move, and the seas will be soon to follow. I shudder to think of the wrath brewing in the water.
>
> Aren was right, it would seem. I would beg his forgiveness, had he not cast himself into mighty Osaigah's clutches an hour ago. My other four Brothers followed. Perhaps they did not wish to partake in such an ending. I cannot find it in my heart to blame them.
>
> I harbour no such fear. I do not know what will take me, but I can feel that I am not long for this world. It is a strange sentiment. I think that I have felt it for quite a while.
>
> I will take my final watch tonight, and see whatever you wish to show me.
>
> Anything.
