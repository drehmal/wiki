# Tenebrae

!!! warning "Side Content"
    This page concerns a piece of side content from the official Drehmal development team which is not a part of any mainline Drehmal map release. It does, however, come from the Drehmal dev team, and can likely be considered fully canon.

--8<-- "am2_spoiler.md"

This book can be found as an item on the ground at the base of Zedari Island, a small isle off the eastern side of [[Sahd]]. It is exclusive to [[Archived_Memory_2|Archived Memory 2: To Feel the Stars]] and cannot be found in the mainline Drehmal map itself. 

The text itself is a short account written by one of the Skyborn monks of the terrible fate that befell their insular community, including a detailed description of the spaghettification what happened to one of the townsfolk.

## Transcription

> As the earth tremored, Inai rang the alarm bell. The rumblings of Dahe are not unknown to us, and upon the alarm we sheltered. Aru's shed sank into the earth as the soil gave way beneath it. Then the tremors ceased.
>
> It hung in the air above the fresh chasm it had carved, as dust rose from the broken earth and fallen blossoms wheeled around the tenebrous globe in rapid spirals. It seemed at once a thing and an absence, both object and void.
>
> The sphere lingered in the air, bobbing in all directions ever so slightly. Inai approached with his glaive and thrust the blade toward it in a cautious prod. His attack glanced off with a metallic clink, and Inai took a slow step forward. At once, a violent force seized the polearm, dragging it towards the sphere, pulling Inai with it. As the blade entered the globe, the shaft began to lengthen and thin. Inai's forward arm followed. He cried out, releasing the glaive from his grip, but it only spun faster, stretching impossibly as it vanished into the dark.
>
> Inai tried to step back, yet his arm stayed outstretched towards the sphere. He dug his heels into the ground, the force dragging him closer still. The moment his hand touched that terrible absence, all of Inai was taken. His body whipped around the sphere in rapid revolution, growing longer and thinner with each transit. His face dragged downward into a grotesque frown, his limbs tangled together like knotted noodles, pink flesh turned to raw red. Inai became thread, then filament, then something finer still until he could no longer be seen. Inai was gone.
>
> Not a word was said as the machine of war lingered in the air, surveying its sudden audience. It turned its featureless form towards me, and... looked at me. In me. And then, in a blink, it sped eastward across the sky, releasing us from its grasp.
>
> Many have already fled to the mainland, fearing that the ocean will soon rip apart in turn. Some tried to give themselves to Osaigah to appease the coming tide.
>
> It will not be enough.

## Extras

"Tenebrae" is Latin for "darkness" or "shadow", likely referring to the pitch black and void-like description of the orb mentioned in this text.
