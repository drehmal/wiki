# Drehmal: PRIME MOTOR OIL

!!! danger "Despite it being decidedly non-canon, this article and the event/map discussed contains spoilers for areas related to post-75% tower completion. If you have not reached 75% completion or finished the full 75% story sequence through the "Apotheosis" advancement, we strongly advise that you do not continue to read this article."

!!! danger "![Spoiler Door](/assets/img/spoiler_door.png)"

**PRIME MOTOR OIL** (stylized as PRIME MΩTOR OIL and acronymized as PMO or PMΩ) follows a joke continuity in which [[Maelihs]] has conquered large areas of the disc and divine moon, not for dominion, but for the construction of massive motorized ice-boat racing tracks. PMO is a complete and official spinoff featuring a proper OST, fully coded racing behavior, and even joke lore books scattered throughout the map.

More information, as well as a download for an offline version of the worldfile, is available at both <https://www.drehmal.net/primemotoroil> and on the [Planet Minecraft thread](https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/drehmal-prime-m-tor-oil-high-octane-ice-boat-racing/).

## Content

Players will spawn in to find themselves in a [[Terminus Network|Terminus]]-like lobby. This lobby contains buttons to start all 4 individual tracks, as well as the Omega Cup Grand Prix, consisting of all four in succession. Various easter eggs and statistics are held in the lobby as well, including mannequins that display the current record times on each track.

There are 4 tracks, each with their own layout and record times to beat. All are versions of current locations from 2.2, but heavily terraformed and altered to better fit the path for the racers. 

Each track, and the grand prix, has a defined set of time splits: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and OMEGA.

### Omega Cup

The Omega Cup is a Grand Prix, consisting of all 4 race tracks in the order presented below. In multiplayer, points are scored Mario Kart-style, based on placement in each individual track.

| **Split** | **Time to Beat** |
|:---------:|:----------------:|
| Bronze |  18:30 |
| Silver | 17:00 |
| Gold | 16:00 |
| OMEGA | 15:17.500 |
